## Mission

Help students capture, organize, and retrieve knowledge effectively — without requiring AI, subscriptions, or external services to deliver core value.

---

## Product Positioning

Studora is **not** a generic note-taking app.  
Studora is **not** an AI wrapper.  
Studora is a **purpose-built academic workspace** designed around real student workflows.

The product fills the gap between:
- Notion (powerful but not student-focused)
- OneNote (student-aware but dated UX)
- Obsidian (powerful but technical, no collaboration)
- Google Keep (too lightweight for serious study)

Studora brings together structured notes, organization by subject, study tools (flashcards, quizzes), task management, and progress tracking in a single coherent workspace.

---

## Non-Goals (Phase 0-11)

- No real-time collaboration
- No AI features (reserved for Phase 12+)
- No mobile native app (PWA improvements in Phase 9)
- No third-party integrations (LMS, Google Classroom, etc.) unless a concrete requirement appears
- No public sharing or publishing
